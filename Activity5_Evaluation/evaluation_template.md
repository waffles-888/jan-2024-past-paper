# Activity 5 – Evaluation

## Instructions

The purpose of this activity is to evaluate the effectiveness of your software solution and reflect on the development process. This is your opportunity to demonstrate your understanding of how well the final product meets the project requirements and identify potential improvements.

You should complete all sections of this document, providing clear explanations and supporting evidence where appropriate.

---

## Task 1: Introduction

Write a brief overview of your project.

Include:

- The name of the software solution.
- The purpose of the program.
- The intended user(s).
- The problem the software was designed to solve.

---

## Task 2: Review the Requirements

For each original project requirement:

1. State the requirement.
2. Explain how your solution meets the requirement.
3. Refer to evidence such as screenshots, code snippets, or testing results.

You should review every requirement identified during the planning stage.

---

## Task 3: Evaluate the Strengths of Your Solution

Identify and explain the strengths of your software.

You may wish to consider:

- Ease of use
- Accuracy of outputs
- Reliability
- Security features
- Input validation
- User interface design
- Efficiency
- Code quality

For each strength:

- Explain why it is a strength.
- Describe how it benefits the user.
- Reference evidence where appropriate.

---

## Task 4: Identify Weaknesses and Limitations

No software solution is perfect. Identify at least three weaknesses or limitations of your program.

For each weakness:

- Explain the issue.
- Describe how it may affect the user.
- Suggest how the issue could be improved in a future version.

---

## Task 5: Evaluate Testing and Debugging

Review the testing completed during Activity 4.

Explain:

- What tests were carried out.
- Any bugs or issues that were identified.
- How the issues were resolved.
- How testing improved the quality and reliability of the final solution.

You should refer to specific examples from your testing evidence.

---

## Task 6: Evaluate the Quality of Your Code

Review the coding standards used throughout development.

Consider:

- Meaningful variable names
- Appropriate comments
- Consistent indentation
- Readability
- Use of functions or procedures
- Maintainability of the code

Explain how these techniques helped produce a quality solution.

---

## Task 7: Future Improvements

Suggest at least three realistic improvements that could be made if additional development time were available.

For each improvement explain:

- What would be added or changed.
- How it would improve the solution.
- Why it would benefit the user.

Possible improvements could include:

- Additional features
- Enhanced security
- Improved accessibility
- A graphical user interface
- Data storage capabilities
- Reporting functionality

---

## Task 8: Conclusion

Write a conclusion that summarises your evaluation.

Your conclusion should consider:

- How successful the project was.
- How well the solution met the requirements.
- The effectiveness of testing.
- The overall quality of the final product.
- Whether the software is suitable for its intended purpose.

---

## Submission Checklist

Before submitting your evaluation, ensure you have included:

- [ ] Introduction
- [ ] Review of all requirements
- [ ] Strengths of the solution
- [ ] Weaknesses and limitations
- [ ] Testing and debugging evaluation
- [ ] Evaluation of code quality
- [ ] Three future improvements
- [ ] Conclusion
- [ ] Supporting evidence where appropriate

## Assessment Tip

The strongest evaluations do more than describe what the software does. They explain why development decisions were made, use evidence from testing and development, and critically assess both the strengths and weaknesses of the final solution.
